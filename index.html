About

Leonardo Cortesia holds a degree in Economics & Digital Management from H-FARM, Europe’s largest startup incubator. His postgraduate education spans Europe, North America, and Asia, where he earned two MBAs and a Master of Arts in Global Sales.

He has earned two podium finishes at the HEC Paris Business Game and authored two academic articles on AI, strategy, and corporate finance. He currently works as a Corporate Development Executive.

He speaks Italian (native), English (C1), German (C1), and Spanish (B2), and has lived across Europe, Latin America, and Southeast Asia.
